President Robert J. Jones, Provost Tricia Serio and more than two dozen new University of Washington faculty toured Washington state last week on the annual Faculty Field Tour . The five-day bus tour departed from the Burke Museum in Seattle on June 15 and made stops at historic sites, the state capitol, health clinics, vineyards, farms, cities and towns throughout the state.
